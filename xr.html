HEADSET REQUIREMENTS

Use a compatible headset browser (e.g., Quest Browser) over HTTPS, and grant immersive VR permission. A desktop WebGL preview works without a headset.

VR INTERACTION

Enter immersive mode, look around the physical-scale scene, and press either controller's trigger to switch realities. Use your headset's menu to exit VR.

COMFORT & SCOPE

No automatic camera movement, artificial locomotion, forced turning, flashing, or jumpscares. This is a small VR scene experiment, not yet the full playable game in VR.
