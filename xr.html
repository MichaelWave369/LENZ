LOADING PORTAL JOURNAL

The same browser save is used in LENZ 2D and VR. Every XR gate crossing persists your new zone, arrival location, and journal. Your evidence and health are preserved.

HEADSET REQUIREMENTS

Use a compatible headset browser (e.g., Quest Browser) over HTTPS, and grant immersive VR permission. A desktop WebGL preview works without a headset.

VR INTERACTION

Face the two numbered gates, aim a controller's pointing ray at either opening, then squeeze its trigger. Aimed gates brighten. Use the headset menu to exit VR. On desktop, use Left Gate / Right Gate buttons.

COMFORT & SCOPE

No forced viewpoint movement, turning, locomotion, flashing, or jump scares. This is still a stationary XR scene. The XR gates share LENZ progress; full 3D exploration, combat and physical clues are not yet implemented.
