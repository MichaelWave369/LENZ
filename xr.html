LOADING PORTAL JOURNAL

The same browser save is used in LENZ 2D and VR. In Frontier, target the glowing scan terminal to gain Case 001's first access code. Visit Dead Sector afterward to use it. Your evidence, health, zone and gate crossings persist on this browser profile.

HEADSET REQUIREMENTS

Use a compatible HTTPS headset browser, such as Quest Browser, and allow immersive VR. The desktop WebGL preview can traverse portals and scan the Frontier terminal using page buttons, but cannot simulate headset teleport targeting.

VR INTERACTION

Point a controller at the floor to choose a bounded teleport spot, then press trigger. A stable cyan square shows the destination. Aim at the Frontier scan console to recover the access code, or at either numbered gate to travel. Desktop buttons provide direct gate and report interactions.

COMFORT & SCOPE

Teleport is the only artificial movement: a discrete viewpoint repositioning with no smooth acceleration or forced turning. Stay inside your real guardian / play boundary. No collisions, climbing, NPC combat, or complete VR campaign are implemented yet. Stop if uncomfortable.
